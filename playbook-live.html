FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What is Playbook Live?

Playbook Live is a division of Her Digital Playbook focused on one-on-one video conversation opportunities and a remote talent network. Depending on the opportunity and eligibility, applicants may apply as Hosts, apply to become a Recruiter, or apply to become a Sub-Agent.

Who can apply?

Adults aged 18 and over who meet the eligibility requirements of the opportunity they apply for. Availability may depend on your country, applicable platform rules and local law.

Is Playbook Live 18+?

Yes. Playbook Live is strictly 18+ only. Applications from anyone under 18 are not accepted.

Do I need previous experience?

Not necessarily. Some opportunities may be suitable for people without previous experience, while others may require relevant skills or experience. Each application is reviewed individually.

Can I work from home?

Yes. Host and Talent Team opportunities are designed to be done remotely, and Sub-Agents build their networks from wherever they are.

Do I need to build a public audience?

No. The Host opportunity is centered around one-on-one video conversations rather than building a public audience.

How does the Host opportunity work?

Hosts take part in one-on-one video conversations with other users on applicable third-party platforms, during hours they choose to be available. Platform requirements, eligibility rules and agency terms apply, and applicants who move to the next step receive the details. Nobody at Playbook Live will ever pressure you to do something you are not comfortable with.

What does a Recruiter do?

Recruiters may help find potential candidates, communicate with and screen candidates, collect applications, arrange interviews and support onboarding. The exact responsibilities depend on the final role and agreement.

What is a Sub-Agent?

A Sub-Agent is an independent network-builder who may identify and introduce qualified hosts to the agency. Sub-Agent opportunities, responsibilities, approval and compensation are subject to applicable agreements.

How are earnings determined?

Earnings and payment arrangements vary based on applicable platform, agency and individual participation terms.

Are earnings guaranteed?

No. Playbook Live does not guarantee income. Earnings, where applicable, depend on individual activity and the terms of the applicable platform and/or agency agreement.

Which countries can participate?

Not every country is eligible — availability depends on platform rules, local law and agency requirements. Our first recruiting focus is the Philippines, with other permitted regions possible in future. Please always apply with accurate information about your country and identity.

How will I know if my application is accepted?

If your application is selected for the next step, we'll contact you using the WhatsApp number or email you provided. We can't promise an individual reply to every application.

What happens after I apply?

Your application is reviewed by the appropriate team. If you are selected for the next stage, we will contact you using the information you provided.
