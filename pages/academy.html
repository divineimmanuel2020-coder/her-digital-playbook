          How learning works here

          1. Learn. Each lesson explains the idea, why it matters, how to do it step by step, and shows a worked example.
          2. Practise. A task for every lesson, a notes space that saves as you type, and a short reflection to make it stick.
          3. Check. A knowledge check after each lesson and a shuffled assessment after each module. Questions you miss go into your review deck.
          4. Build. Real projects and a capstone you assess against a rubric.
          5. Certify. Pass everything with 70% or more overall and earn a certificate: Pass, Merit (80%) or Distinction (90%).

          About your certificate

          • You choose the name printed on it, once, and it is locked afterwards.
          • The exact date, time and time zone of issue are printed on it.
          • Download it as a PDF or image, print it, or share it to LinkedIn, WhatsApp, X, Facebook, Telegram or email.
          • Every certificate has a verification link that anyone can open to check the details.
          • Your progress and certificates are saved in your browser on this device.
