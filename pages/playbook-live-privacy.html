Playbook Live Privacy Policy

How we handle information submitted through Playbook Live applications.

Applies to Playbook Live, a division of Her Digital Playbook. This is separate from, and supplements, our general Her Digital Playbook Privacy Policy.

What We Collect

When you apply to Playbook Live as a Host, Recruiter, Virtual Assistant, or Sub-Agent, we collect the information you provide in your application, which may include: your name, email address, WhatsApp number, country, age confirmation, previous experience, availability, your application answers, an introduction video, social or agency links, expected compensation, and basic technical submission information (such as the time you applied).

Why We Collect It

We use this information to: review and process your application; communicate with you about your application; conduct recruitment and onboarding; manage our talent network; maintain safety and prevent abuse; comply with applicable legal obligations; and other legitimate business purposes connected to operating Playbook Live.

Introduction Videos

Introduction videos are stored in a private storage system that is not publicly accessible. Videos are only viewable through secure, time-limited links used internally to review applications. We do not publish, share, or use your video for marketing without your separate consent.

Who Can See Your Information

Your application is reviewed by the appropriate Playbook Live team. We do not create a public applicant directory, and your application is never indexed by search engines or made publicly accessible.

Service Providers

To operate the application system, your information may be processed by the infrastructure providers that power this website, which currently include Vercel (hosting), Supabase (secure application storage), and Resend (email delivery). These providers process data on our behalf and do not independently use it for their own purposes.

Data Retention

We aim to retain application information only for as long as reasonably necessary for recruitment, legal, or operational purposes, and not indefinitely without reason. Older applications may be periodically reviewed and removed where they're no longer needed.

Your Choices

You can contact us at any time to ask about the information we hold about your application, or to request that it be deleted, through our Contact page. We'll respond to reasonable requests, though some information may need to be retained where we have a legitimate business or legal reason to do so.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect application data, including private storage, restricted access, and secure transmission. However, no system can be guaranteed to be 100% secure, and we can't promise absolute security of any information you submit.

18+ Only

Playbook Live opportunities are for adults aged 18 and over only. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18, and applications confirming an age under 18 are not accepted.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as Playbook Live grows. We'll update the date on this page whenever meaningful changes are made.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy or your application data? Reach out through our Contact page.
