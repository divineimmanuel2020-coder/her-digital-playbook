Playbook Live Safety & Conduct

Our standards for a safe, respectful Playbook Live community.

Applies to everyone who applies to, or participates in, Playbook Live — Hosts, Recruiters, Virtual Assistants, and Sub-Agents.

Playbook Live is built around real conversations and real opportunities. That only works in an environment that's safe, honest, and respectful — for applicants, for participants, and for everyone they interact with. This policy sets out what we expect, and how to reach us if something isn't right.

18+ Only

Playbook Live is strictly for adults aged 18 and over. We do not accept applications from minors, and any account or application later found to involve a minor will be removed immediately.

We Do Not Tolerate

The following are never acceptable in connection with Playbook Live, and will result in an application being rejected or a participant's involvement being terminated:

  • Involvement of minors in any capacity
  • Coercion, threats, or forced participation
  • Trafficking or exploitation of any kind
  • Fraud or impersonation
  • Harassment, blackmail, or abusive behavior
  • Illegal activity of any kind
  • Deceptive recruitment practices
  • Manipulation or discrimination
  • Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith

Honesty in Applications

We rely on applicants to provide truthful information, including their real age, country, and identity. Misrepresenting any of these — including to try to bypass geographic or eligibility restrictions — is not permitted.

Platform Rules Apply

Where an opportunity involves a third-party platform, participants are expected to follow that platform's own rules and policies at all times, alongside this policy.

How to Report a Concern

If you experience or witness anything that concerns you — whether it's about another participant, an application, or anything else connected to Playbook Live — please tell us through our Contact page. We take every report seriously and will not retaliate against anyone who reports a genuine concern in good faith.

Our Commitment

We aim to build Playbook Live as a professional, respectful, and legitimate opportunity for everyone involved. This policy exists to protect that — not to make participation feel frightening, but to make clear what we stand for.
