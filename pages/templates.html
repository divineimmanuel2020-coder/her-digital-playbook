Free Girlie Templates

Fill it in. Copy it. Use it. 🎀

Scripts, trackers, checklists and worksheets for the real-life moments, free and ready to go.

22 ready-to-use scripts, checklists, trackers and worksheets for freelancing, careers, money, content and business. Fill them in, see a live preview, then copy, download or print.

Templates:

Freelancing

  • Cold Pitch Message: A short, specific message to a business you would love to work with. Tips: Name one real thing you noticed about their business. Make one small, specific offer rather than "I can do anything". Send ten and expect a few replies. That is normal.
  • Simple Project Proposal: One clear page that covers the goal, scope, price, timeline and next step. Tips: List what is NOT included. That is where most arguments start. State revision rounds as a number. Always include a payment schedule, and ask for a deposit.
  • Client Onboarding Checklist: Everything to set up before you start working, so projects begin smoothly. Tips: Send it to the client as a welcome message. Do not start until the first three items are ticked.
  • Scope Change Request: Reply kindly and firmly when "just one more thing" becomes extra work. Tips: Say yes to the person and name the extra clearly. Give a price and a date so the decision is easy.
  • Payment Reminder (3 stages): Three polite-but-firm follow-ups for late invoices, from gentle to final. Tips: Send the first reminder the day after it is due. Always attach the invoice again. Keep a record of every message.
  • Rate Increase Email: Raise your price without grovelling. Warm, plain and confident. Tips: Give 30 days notice. Do not apologise for the number. Offer a reduced-scope option if budget is tight.
  • Testimonial Request: Ask for a testimonial in a way that makes it easy to say yes. Tips: Ask right after a happy moment. Give two or three prompts so they know what to write.
  • Discovery Call Questions: Questions to ask a new client so you scope and price properly. Tips: Tick off the questions as you get answers. Write answers beneath each question in your notes.
  • Scope of Work Checklist: Check you have covered everything before you say "yes" to a project. Tips: If you cannot tick it, ask the client. Use it as the structure for your proposal.
  • Portfolio Case Study Outline: Turn a project (even a practice one) into a case study that sells your skills. Tips: A practice project is a legitimate case study if you label it honestly. Show your thinking, not just the final result.

Career

  • Job Application Tracker: Keep every application, its status and your follow-up dates in one place. Tips: Add a row the same day you apply. Follow up 5 to 7 working days after applying.
  • Resume Bullet Rewriter: Turn duties into results using the action + what + outcome formula. Tips: Start with a strong verb. Add a number whenever you honestly can. Never exaggerate. Interviewers will ask about it.
  • Salary Negotiation Script: Words for asking for more, whether it is a new offer or your annual review. Tips: Practise out loud once. Anchor with a number, not a feeling. After you ask, stop talking and let them respond.
  • Interview Prep Worksheet: Prepare three stories that answer most interview questions. Tips: Use the STAR structure: Situation, Task, Action, Result. Keep each story under two minutes.
  • LinkedIn Profile Checklist: A quick audit to make your profile clear and findable. Tips: Fix the headline first. It shows everywhere.

Money

  • Income & Expense Tracker: Log money in and money out, and see your totals instantly. Tips: Log it the day it happens. Check the profit line monthly.
  • Monthly Money Check-in: Fifteen minutes each month to see what happened and what to do next. Tips: Do it on the same day each month. Keep it kind. The aim is clarity, not guilt.
  • 90-Day Money Goal Planner: Turn a money goal into a number per month and a first step. Tips: Choose a goal that is specific and has a date. Make the first step small enough for this week.

Content

  • 30-Day Content Planner: Plan a month of posts with a topic, format and status for each day. Tips: Batch your content on one day per week. Repeat what worked.

Business

  • Offer Builder: Turn a skill into a clear offer people understand and can buy. Tips: An offer names who it is for and the outcome, not just the activity.
  • Business Idea Check: Pressure-test an idea before you spend time or money on it. Tips: If you cannot name ten potential customers, find them first. Look for people who have already paid for something similar.
  • Customer Interview Worksheet: Questions that uncover what people really want, without leading them. Tips: Ask about the past, not the future. "Last time you tried X, what happened?" Listen far more than you talk.

Questions people ask

Are the templates really free?

Yes. You can fill them in, copy them, download them or print them, with no account.

Is what I type saved?

It autosaves in your own browser so you can come back to it. Nothing is sent to us. Clear a template any time with the Clear button.

Can I use these for clients?

Yes. The scripts and checklists are starting points. Adjust them to your situation, and for contracts or legal matters, get advice where you live.
