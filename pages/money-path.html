Money Path

Find your money path 💸

Not every direction suits every girl. Tell us your time, budget and strengths, and see what fits, honestly.

Nine questions about your goal, time, budget, strengths and preferences. Then Money Path ranks 14 real digital income directions, explains why each fits (or does not), shows a cautious early income range, and gives you a first step and a 30-day plan.

Directions compared:

  • 🗂️ Virtual Assistant: Be the person who keeps a busy founder organised: inbox, calendar, bookings, admin. Typical first paid work in 2 to 8 weeks.
  • 📱 Social Media Management: Plan, write and schedule posts for small businesses that have no time for it. Typical first paid work in 3 to 10 weeks.
  • 🎨 Graphic Design: Create logos, social graphics, brand kits and simple print pieces for small brands. Typical first paid work in 3 to 10 weeks.
  • ✍️ Copywriting: Write websites, emails, product pages and ads that make people act. Typical first paid work in 3 to 12 weeks.
  • 🎬 Short-Form Video Editing: Turn raw footage into tight Reels, Shorts and TikToks with captions and sound. Typical first paid work in 3 to 10 weeks.
  • 🤳 UGC Creator: Make authentic product videos and photos that brands use in their own ads. Typical first paid work in 2 to 10 weeks.
  • 🛍️ Digital Products: Make a template, planner or mini-guide once and sell it again and again. Typical first paid work in 4 to 16 weeks.
  • 💌 Email Marketing Support: Write and send newsletters and automations for businesses that already have a list. Typical first paid work in 4 to 12 weeks.
  • 🎧 Remote Customer Support: Help customers by chat and email for a company, often on a set schedule. Typical first paid work in 2 to 8 weeks.
  • 🎓 Online Tutoring & Coaching: Teach a subject or skill you know well, one-to-one or in small groups. Typical first paid work in 1 to 6 weeks.
  • 🔍 SEO & Blog Writing: Write helpful articles that rank in search for businesses that want steady traffic. Typical first paid work in 4 to 12 weeks.
  • 💻 No-Code Website Builds: Build simple websites and landing pages with drag-and-drop tools for small businesses. Typical first paid work in 4 to 14 weeks.
  • 🤖 AI-Assisted Operations: Set up AI tools and simple automations that save small teams hours each week. Typical first paid work in 3 to 10 weeks.
  • 📹 Faceless Content Creator: Build an audience with videos, pins or posts, then earn from ads, affiliates and products. Typical first paid work in 12 to 40 weeks.

What Money Path asks

  1. What monthly income would feel like a real win?
  2. How many hours a week can you really give this?
  3. What can you spend to get started?
  4. How soon do you need the first money?
  5. Pick up to three things you are naturally good at.
  6. How much talking to people do you want in your work?
  7. How do you feel about being on camera?
  8. What kind of income shape suits you?
  9. Where are you starting from?

Your answers stay on your device. Results are a starting point for thinking, not financial advice.

Questions people ask

How does Money Path pick directions for me?

Every direction is scored out of 100 from four things: how well it uses your strengths (34%), whether your hours, budget and timeline are enough (30%), whether it matches the people contact, camera use and income shape you want (20%), and whether your goal is realistic (16%). The reasons you see are generated from those same numbers.

Are the income figures real?

They are cautious illustrations for beginners at around months 3 to 4, including unpaid time spent finding clients and learning. Many people earn less at first, some earn more. They are not promises.

What if I have no experience at all?

Choose "Brand new". The learning curve of each direction is taken into account, and every result includes a first step and a 30-day plan that assume you are starting from zero.

Why does it sometimes suggest pairing two directions?

Some routes, like digital products or content, pay slowly. If you want money sooner, pairing one with a faster route can give you cash flow while the slower one grows.
