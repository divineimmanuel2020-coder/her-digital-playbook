Game Room

The game room 🎮

Quick rounds that sharpen your money sense, client judgement and scam radar.

Six short games turn the decisions freelancers and online earners face into quick rounds, each with a clear explanation of why an answer works. Play the daily challenge to build a streak.

Games:

  • 💸 Money Moves: Make seven money decisions and see what they do to your safety net, growth and calm. Each choice changes three meters. There is rarely one perfect answer, so you are watching the trade-offs. Keep all three above 25 to finish strong.
  • 🚩 Red Flag Radar: Is that client message healthy or a warning sign? Build a streak. Read each message and decide: green flag or red flag. Streaks add bonus points. Healthy messages are in the mix too, so do not flag everything.
  • 🧩 Skill Match: Match each business need with the digital skill that solves it. Read the need, pick the skill that fits best. Explanations tell you what each skill really does.
  • 📥 Inbox Boss: Triage a busy freelancer inbox. Reply, clarify, quote, decline or report. Choose the best way to handle each message. The best action earns 2 points, an acceptable one earns 1.
  • 🏷️ Price This: Set a price for real-looking projects and learn the formula behind it. You get the hours and the hourly rate. Then decide on the price, including buffer time, rush fees and usage rights. Within 15% is a bullseye.
  • 🕵️ Spot the Scam: Tap the warning signs in messages, then give your verdict. Tap any phrase that looks suspicious, then choose scam or legit. You score for the right verdict and for finding the real signs without false alarms.

Questions people ask

Do the games change every time?

Yes. Each game draws a fresh random set from a larger pool, and the answer order is shuffled. The daily challenge is the same five questions for everyone on the same day.

How is my score and mastery worked out?

Each game ends with a score out of 100. Your best score sets your mastery tier: Bronze at 50, Silver at 70 and Gold at 90.

Can I farm XP by replaying?

No. The first finish of each game pays XP once and beating your personal best pays a little more. After that, replaying is for practice.
