Your personal route

Start here, girl.

Five quick questions. One clear route through the Playground, built around your goal and your time.

Five quick questions: where you are, what you want, how much time you have, what is in your way and how you like to learn. Then you get a route of up to five steps, a plan for your first week, and a few templates, terms and reads that fit.

The five questions are: Where are you right now? What do you most want right now? How much time can you give each week? What is in your way? Pick up to two. How do you like to learn?

  • Money PathExplore income directions that fit your time, skills and budget, and get a practical first step.
  • Client SimulatorPractise real freelance moments: scope, pricing, payment and difficult conversations.
  • Game RoomSix mini-games for money decisions, client judgement, pricing and spotting scams.

Questions people ask

How does Start Here choose my route?

Each of your answers adds points to the experiences that help most with it. The route is those experiences in order, and the reasons shown are the real reasons that added the points.

Can I change my answers?

Yes. Retake it any time. Your latest route is saved on this device and used to suggest what to do next across the Playground.

What happens to my answers?

They stay in your browser. They are also used to pre-fill your hours and goal in Money Path, so you do not have to enter them twice.
