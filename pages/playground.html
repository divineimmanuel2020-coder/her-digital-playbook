How the Playground works

Reading about money, clients and digital skills is useful. Practising them is what makes them stick. The Playground gives you safe places to try things before the stakes are real: you can compare income routes, rehearse a difficult client conversation, test your instincts in short games, and use ready-made templates for the real thing.

  • Money Path: It compares income directions against your time, budget and skills, and gives you a practical first step.
  • Client Simulator: It lets you practise client conversations, with real consequences, before they happen for real.
  • Game Room: It turns money, pricing and scam-spotting decisions into short games with explanations.
  • Start Here: It asks a few questions about your goal and time, then gives you a route through the Playground.
  • Free Girlie Templates: It has trackers, worksheets, checklists and scripts you can fill in and use straight away.
  • Digital Girl Glossary: It explains digital and business terms in plain language, with examples you can use.

Everything is built to be honest. Scores are explained, advice has trade-offs, and anything that looks like income is labelled as an illustration. You are always the one deciding what is right for your life.

Questions people ask

Is the Playground free?

Yes. Every experience in the Playground is free to use, with no account needed. Your progress is saved in your own browser.

Where do I begin if I am not sure?

Start Here asks five quick questions about your goal, time and what is in your way, then builds a route through the Playground for you.

Does the Playground guarantee that I will earn money?

No. It is a place to practise, compare options and build confidence. Income examples are cautious illustrations for beginners, never promises, and real results depend on your market, effort and consistency.

Is my information collected?

The answers you give stay on your device. Nothing you type into the Playground is sent anywhere, and the reply checker in the Client Simulator runs entirely in your browser.

What are XP, levels and badges?

Finishing experiences earns XP and badges, shared with the rest of the site. They are there to make practice more fun and to show you how far you have come.
